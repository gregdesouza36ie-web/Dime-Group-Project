Innovation Details

ÉireID provides Irish residents with a unified digital identity platform that securely connects multiple public and private services in one place. Unlike existing systems that focus primarily on authentication. ÉireID focuses on everyday usability by combining digital identity, transport access, document storage, and service verification into a single mobile application.
This reduces administrative friction, improves accessibility, and enables faster identity verification across both government and private services.
ÉireID is not only a digital app for the Irish residents, but also a platform that connects users to a wide range of government and administrative services, allowing citizens to complete many official processes digitally in the app without any need to visit offices or manage multiple websites. This app integrates numerous public and private services that are commonly required in everyday life.
The core innovation of ÉireID is combining digital identity, NFC verification, document storage, and service access into a single ecosystem.

Key features

Location Based Suggestion:
This feature intelligently displays relevant documents when needed, such as automatically showing a Leap Card at a bus stop or a boarding pass and passport when at an airport
Face ID Authentication and two-step authentication:
Are used for fast and secure access, controlling that just verified users can use the app
NFC:
Is generated for each document, providing an easy way to verify identity in person in administrative offices etc. The token allows authorised institutions, such as Gardaí, banks, government offices, or transport inspectors, to verify a person’s identity by simply tapping the user’s phone with an NFC reader.
User support system:
The app provides a comprehensive help and support system, including the digital handbook, online chat assistant , or chat bot powered by AI
Account management
It allows users to manage privacy, security, check account details safely
Languages:
Available in all languages.
All documents are dublicated in Irish and English
