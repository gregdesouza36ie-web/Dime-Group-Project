Innovation Details

ÉireID is not only a digital app for the Irish residents, but also a platform that connects users to a wide range of government and administrative services, allowing citizens to complete many official processes digitally in the app without any need to visit offices or manage multiple websites. This app integrates numerous public and private services that are commonly required in everyday life.
The core innovation of ÉireID is combining digital identity, NFC verification, document storage, and service access into a single ecosystem.

Services included

Document storage:
Passport, Driving licence, Public Service Card, Certificate of Birth, Certificate of Marriage/Divorce, Certificate of Inheritance, Medical Card etc.
Transport:
includes integration with public transport such as Leap cards and digital tickets
Legal services:
Digital signing of legal documents: for example, power attorneys etc
Healthcare:
Secure storage medical card, prescriptions, and healthcare verifications
Tax and financial:
Interactions with Revenue systems for identifications, including paying taxes straightaway in app
Voting and citizenship services:
Digital verifications for personal voting or other government processes.
Housing and property services:
Manages rental and property documentations
Education services:
Includes students verifications, scholarships, grants, access to the university sites
Vehicle services:
Registration of vehicles, licence renewals, payments of traffic fines

How it works?

1. Register and Verify Identity
2. Documents stored securely
3. NFC used for verification:
4. Face ID and Authenticator protect data
5. Connected via APIs

Target users

Irish residents, businesses, and public organisations needing secure identity verification.
