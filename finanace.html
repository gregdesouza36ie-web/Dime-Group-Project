Revenue Generation

EireId uses two main business models to
generate revenue

Freemium Model

Users can access essential digital ID features for free, ensuring mass adoption. Premium plans unlock advanced security, cloud backup, and priority verification.

Subscription Model

Monthly and yearly subscriptions provide a steady revenue stream. Affordable pricing ensures accessibility while maintaining profitability.

Financial Overview

The estimated total cost for the first year of development and operation is €432,000.

This includes:

  • Development costs: €250,000 (app development, system infrastructure, design, and security testing)
  • Marketing costs: €50,000 (social media, branding, and advertising)
  • Operational costs: €110,000 (cloud hosting, maintenance, and customer support)
  • Additional costs: €22,000 (app store fees and data protection compliance)

These costs reflect the investment required to develop, launch, and maintain the digital identity platform.
