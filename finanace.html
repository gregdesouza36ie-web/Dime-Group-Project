Competitor Comparison

Features ✓ ÉireID Competitors
Purpose Acts as a secure digital wallet providing access to a multitude of services and documents all in one place. Similar apps such as MyGovID don’t provide direct access to personal documents and services, it only acts as an access portal. Platforms as Google Wallet and Apple wallet mainly focus on payments and have limited identity and government integration.
Documents Provides direct storage and access to documnets such as your passport or driver's licence. MyGovID doesn’t act as a document wallet like EireID does. It only uses your passport during verification, not as a stored ID.
Services Has services like Revenue, Health, Student Grants, and transportation cards and tickets embedded into the app. Acts as a gateway login to services such as; Welfare payments, tax services and job applications.
Verification Instant verification through key features such as Face ID and NFC Verification. Multi-step process through the requirement of needing a Public Services Card and verified mobile phone number.
